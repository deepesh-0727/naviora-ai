## Your hospital visit, made easier

- Book an appointment by speaking or using the app's available booking options.
- Check your place in the queue from your phone.
- Send an emergency alert to the care team using **SOS**. For immediate danger, also call the hospital emergency number.

## Set up your account

1. Open the app and choose **Register**.
2. Enter the details requested by the hospital and your mobile number.
3. Enter the one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone.
4. When setup is complete, sign in and follow the on-screen instructions.

If the OTP does not arrive, check your phone number and network, then request another code. Ask reception for help if you still cannot sign in.

## During your visit

**Book by voice:** Open Voice Assistant, allow microphone access, then press and hold the microphone while speaking. Review the proposed appointment and confirm it before it is booked. You can use the app's other booking option if voice is unavailable.

**Check the queue:** Open Dashboard to see your current queue position. The number may change as staff complete visits.

**Ask for urgent help:** Press and hold **SOS** and follow the confirmation shown in the app. If the alert does not confirm, contact hospital staff directly or call the hospital emergency number.
