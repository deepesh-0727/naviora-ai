## 3. Data handling

Before any real patient data is used, the parties must document the permitted data, purpose, access roles, retention period, incident contacts, and deletion/return process in a signed data-processing or equivalent agreement approved by counsel. The parties must confirm the technical and organizational safeguards against the actual deployed configuration and the Hospital's policies. Do not treat this template as evidence of a particular certification or regulatory compliance status.

Access should be limited to authorized personnel and activity should be audited where the deployed system supports it. At the Hospital's written request, Provider will coordinate return or deletion of pilot data within **30 days**, subject to applicable law, backup retention, and a mutually agreed verification process. **Counsel must specify exceptions, backup handling, and the responsible party for each data category.**

## 4. Service availability

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the pilot service available. The proposed **99.5% availability target is best-effort only**, measured monthly, excluding planned maintenance, third-party outages, network/connectivity outside Provider's control, and force majeure. This is not a service-level guarantee unless a separately signed service-level schedule says otherwise. The Hospital should maintain its existing procedures for care delivery and emergencies if the service is unavailable.

## 5. Safety and responsibility

The service provides administrative workflow and decision-support features. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and must not be the sole basis for a clinical decision or emergency response. Hospital personnel remain responsible for clinical decisions, patient care, and following the Hospital's emergency protocols. Each party will promptly notify the other of a suspected safety or security incident through the contacts above. **Counsel must draft the final allocation of responsibility, indemnities, liability caps, and insurance requirements.**

## 6. Success criteria

The parties will review the measures in [Pilot Success Metrics](success-metrics.md), including data availability and sample size. Before launch, record baseline measures, targets, metric owners, the review period, and any exclusions here: **[attach or complete agreed measurement plan]**. A metric with no observations will be reported as unavailable/no data, not as zero or as evidence of success.

## 7. Fees and expenses

Proposed pilot fee: **No charge for the pilot period**, subject to written confirmation by both parties. Fees, support levels, hosting, integrations, taxes, and pricing after the pilot are **to be agreed in a separate written document**. Neither party is committed to a post-pilot purchase by participating in this evaluation.

## 8. Ending the pilot and handling data

Either party may propose ending the pilot by giving **[number] days' written notice**. Either party may request an immediate pause if it reasonably believes continued operation creates a safety, privacy, or security risk. The parties will coordinate service shutdown, user access, export/return, and deletion under the final data agreement. The target is to complete agreed data return or deletion within 30 days after the end date, subject to legal retention duties and the terms counsel approves.
